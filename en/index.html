Scope of Russian and Caucasus Studies

An interdisciplinary perspective on the region and its relationships

Russian and Caucasus Studies welcomes scholarly research on languages and linguistics, history, society, literature, culture, politics, economics and international relations concerning Russia and the Caucasus. Its scope also includes regional and comparative studies of identity, migration, religion, media, cultural heritage, diplomacy, security and cross-border cooperation.

The journal welcomes original, well-documented and interdisciplinary research with a clear research question, appropriate methodology and identifiable scholarly contribution, advancing understanding of Russia, the Caucasus and their place in regional and global affairs.
