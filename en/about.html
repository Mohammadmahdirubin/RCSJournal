About Russian and Caucasus Studies

Official identity, mission, aims, audience and scholarly scope of the journal.

Introduction

Russian and Caucasus Studies (Persian: مطالعات روسیه و قفقاز) is a specialized journal in the field of Russian language studies and related scholarly areas. It aims to provide a structured, transparent and academic platform for the publication of original research and to strengthen connections among researchers, teachers, translators and students.

Consistent with its title, Russian and Caucasus Studies covers multidisciplinary research on Russia, the Caucasus and their regional connections. The journal welcomes scholarship on languages and linguistics, literature, culture, history, society, politics, economics, international relations, regional security, migration, identity, religion, media, translation and intercultural communication. Comparative and interdisciplinary studies are also welcome when they present a clear research question and a defensible scholarly method.

Mission

The journal’s mission is to provide a scholarly platform for studying Russia and the Caucasus through linguistic, historical, cultural, social, political and international perspectives. It seeks to publish rigorous, well-documented research that improves understanding of the region’s societies, languages, cultures, institutions and regional relationships.

Aims

  • Develop and publish original research in Russian language and related studies.
  • Strengthen scholarly research on teaching Russian as a foreign language and teacher education.
  • Expand linguistic, literary, cultural and translation studies concerning Russia and the Russian-speaking world.
  • Encourage comparative and interdisciplinary research between Russian and Persian and other related fields.
  • Foster scholarly dialogue among researchers inside and outside Iran.
  • Emphasize research methodology, transparent argument and accurate use of scholarly sources.
  • Build an organized and accessible archive of research published in Russian studies.

Audience

The journal’s primary audience includes researchers in linguistics and Russian language, professors and teachers of Russian, researchers in Russian literature and culture, specialists in translation and translation studies, postgraduate students, and researchers working from comparative and interdisciplinary perspectives on the Russian-speaking world.

Scope

Languages and linguistics

Russian and other regional languages, linguistics, sociolinguistics, language education, translation and intercultural communication.

History and society

Russian and Caucasian history, social change, demography, migration, identity, religion, institutions and everyday life.

Literature and culture

Literature, arts, cultural heritage, collective memory, media, cinema, popular culture and comparative cultural studies.

Politics and governance

Political thought, domestic politics, governance systems, public policy, parties, movements and political change.

International relations and security

Foreign policy, diplomacy, regional organizations, security, conflicts, international cooperation and Russia–Caucasus relations with neighboring states.

Economics and regional studies

Political economy, trade, energy, development, geopolitics, cross-border connections and interdisciplinary research on Russia and the Caucasus.

Accepted manuscript types

The journal considers Original Research Articles and Review Articles / Book Reviews, provided they fit the journal’s scope and meet its scholarly and ethical requirements. The submission type selected in the form must accurately reflect the manuscript.
